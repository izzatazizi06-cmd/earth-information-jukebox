THE MISSION

Science is for everyone.

Created as an independent prototype inspired by the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge: The Earth Information Jukebox. This project demonstrates real-time browser sonification, an accessible chart, and CSV import.

Transparency: The Global Temperature mode uses real NASA GISTEMP v4 annual global land–ocean temperature anomalies (1880–2025, relative to 1951–1980). NASA data source. Ocean and Ice modes and globe artwork remain illustrative, not NASA Earth Information Center frames or satellite measurements. The sonification is synthetic and is not a recording of Earth.
